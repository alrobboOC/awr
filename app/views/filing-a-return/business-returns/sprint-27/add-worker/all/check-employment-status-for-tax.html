Before you add a worker

Check employment status for tax

Before you add a worker to your return, you need to make sure they are self-employed for tax purposes for the work they do for you.

Why you need to check

You are responsible for checking each worker’s employment status. Someone who is self-employed for other work may be employed for the work they do for you.

If you treat a worker as self-employed when they should be employed, you may have to pay unpaid tax and National Insurance, as well as penalties.

Use the employment status check to help you decide whether the worker should be included on your return. If they are employed for tax purposes, their payments should be handled through PAYE instead.

Who can use the tool

Businesses, workers and agents can use CEST. Its result informs who handles tax and National Insurance.
