You have no authorised clients

You do not have any clients authorised to use the Business Returns Service (BRS) service.

To file returns for a client, they must authorise you to act on their behalf.

What you need to do

Ask your client to authorise you. They can do this by either:

  • authorising you through their HMRC online account
  • downloading and completing form 64-8 to authorise you by post

It can take up to 5 working days for new authorisations to appear in your account.
