Verification of worker

Start worker verification journey

Chrissie Watkins is a business who is about to start a business project that will involve a total of 7 workers. 4 of these workers are brand new, so they will need to be verified.

The other 3 businesss already exist in her worker list. However, because they have not been added to a BRS return for 5 years, they have lost their verification status and she would like to re-verify them to ensure that the correct deduction rate is being applied.

The 4 brand new workers who need to be verified are

  • Martin Brody (Individual)
  • Quint Transportation (Company)
  • Hooper and Associates (Partnership)
  • The Kintner Group (Trust)

The 3 existing businesss who will also be added to this verification request are:

  • InGen Research (Company)
  • Alan Grant (Individual)
  • Malcolm and Sattler (Partnership)

Turns out that the 3 existing businesss are all missing a UTR, so they are flagged as 'Insufficient' during the verification flow.

Chrissie decides to remove InGen from this request, proceed with verifying Malcom and Sattler (despite the effect on their tax treatment), and amend Alan Grant's worker information to include a UTR
