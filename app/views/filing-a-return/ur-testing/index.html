Start subcontractor verification journey

Chrissie Watkins is a contractor who is about to start a construction project that will involve a total of 7 subcontractors. 4 of these subcontractors are brand new, so they will need to be verified.

The other 3 contractors already exist in her subcontractor list. However, because they have not been added to a CIS return for 5 years, they have lost their verification status and she would like to re-verify them to ensure that the correct deduction rate is being applied.

The 4 brand new subcontractors who need to be verified are

  • Martin Brody (Individual)
  • Quint Transportation (Company)
  • Hooper and Associates (Partnership)
  • The Kintner Group (Trust)

The 3 existing contractors who will also be added to this verification request are:

  • InGen Research (Company)
  • Alan Grant (Individual)
  • Malcolm and Sattler (Partnership)

Turns out that the 3 existing contractors are all missing a UTR, so they are flagged as 'Insufficient' during the verification flow.

Chrissie decides to remove InGen from this request, proceed with verifying Malcom and Sattler (despite the effect on their tax treatment), and amend Alan Grant's subcontractor information to include a UTR
