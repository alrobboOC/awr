Reverify existing subcontractors

To make sure that their tax treatment is correct and up to date, you can reverify any of your existing subcontractors.

You may want to reverify subcontractors if they:

  • have recently changed any of their information
  • have not been included on a return recently and have lost their verified status
  • are about to lose their verified status
