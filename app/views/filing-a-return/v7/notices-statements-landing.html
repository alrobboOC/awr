Understanding your notices

Confirmation statements

You'll receive a confirmation statement when HMRC accepts your CIS return. This confirms the return has been processed.

Penalty warnings

HMRC will send you a penalty warning if your return is late or if there are errors. The notice will explain what you need to do.

General notices

HMRC may send you general notices about changes to CIS, important reminders, or requests for additional information.

How long to keep notices

You should keep all CIS notices and statements for at least 3 years after the end of the tax year they relate to.
